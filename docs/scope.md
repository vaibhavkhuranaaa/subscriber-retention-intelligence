# Scope

## Decisions

- What changed in active subscribers, gross receipts, renewals, cancellations, and churn?
- Which cohort, plan, payment, registration, tenure, and listening segments retain best?
- Which subscribers fit finite outreach capacity at a selected cutoff?
- Does a calibrated challenger beat the named baseline on future labels?
- Under explicit assumed lift and costs, what threshold has the strongest simulated value?

## Included

- Full detailed subscription transactions and subscriber-day listening.
- Subscription-state reconstruction and cohort retention.
- Time-safe churn modeling and threshold economics.
- Private journey and model explanation.
- Public responsive product, dbt lineage, CI, recovery, and temporary Fabric plus browser-authored Power BI evidence.

## Excluded

- Enterprise SaaS accounts, contracts, seats, ARR, MRR, or expansion.
- Recognized revenue or accounting treatment.
- Support or CRM workflows.
- Causal intervention lift.
- Real-time ingestion.
