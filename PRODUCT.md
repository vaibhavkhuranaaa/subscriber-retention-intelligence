# Product

## Name

Subscriber Retention Intelligence

## User

Subscription retention and lifecycle leaders allocating finite outreach capacity.

## Workflow

A weekly review begins with subscriber and gross-receipt movement, diagnoses renewal, cancellation, cohort, plan, payment, acquisition, and listening behavior, evaluates calibrated future churn risk, and selects a capacity threshold. A separate scenario view applies explicit assumed lift and costs.

## Capabilities

- Detailed membership and subscription-transaction reconstruction.
- Subscriber-day listening analysis.
- Renewal, cancellation, challenge-compatible churn, and cohort retention.
- Plan, payment method, auto-renewal, registration source, tenure, and engagement segmentation.
- Time-safe logistic baseline and calibrated nonlinear challenger.
- Capacity, threshold, and simulated intervention economics.
- Public aggregate and privacy-reviewed derived analysis plus private pseudonymous drill-down.
- Governed web and DuckDB/dbt delivery, plus temporary Fabric and browser-authored Power BI evidence.

## Principles

- Consumer subscription is not enterprise SaaS.
- Gross receipts are not recognized revenue.
- Expiration, active status, and renewal are reconstructed and visibly defined.
- Observed, modeled, and simulated values remain distinct.
- Aggregates accelerate detailed facts but never replace them.
