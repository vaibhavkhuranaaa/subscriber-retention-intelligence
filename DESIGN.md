# Subscriber Retention Intelligence design system

## World

Operate-mode retention analysis blotter used under ordinary office light. Near-black navy headers, neutral white working fields, cool blue-gray rules, tabular measures, one amber adverse signal, and one green validation signal. Dense, square, and explicit about comparison windows, denominators, and evidence basis. The product should resemble durable analytical software, never a presentation template.

## Hierarchy

1. Material movements with current, comparison, absolute change, relative change, and evidence basis.
2. Selected movement reconciliation, definition, cutoff, and limitation.
3. Matching cohort and segment breaks with real denominators.
4. Private subscriber journey, calibrated score, threshold, and scenario assumptions.
5. Grain, lineage, ownership, export, and source boundary.

## Components

Module bar, comparison rail, movement blotter, selected-evidence inspector, numerator-denominator reconciliation, two-window plot, cohort break table, segment break table, dimension tabs, private subscriber journey, calibration view, threshold control, scenario decision plot, assumption console, definition register, and honest loading, empty, stale, permission, and provider-error states.

## Behavior

Selecting a movement updates its plot, arithmetic basis, definition, and limitation without navigation. Dimension tabs update comparable segment rows. Filters must affect a decision: window, cohort, plan, payment method, auto-renewal, registration source, threshold, and capacity. Scenario outputs remain labeled simulated.

## Composition

The application shell uses a compact dark product bar, horizontal module navigation, and a ruled comparison strip. Overview splits into a wide movement table and a fixed analytical inspector, followed by synchronized diagnostic tables. Tables scroll inside their own regions on narrow screens. Scenario planning retains its assumption console. Avoid heroes, KPI cards, floating panels, decorative icons, gradients, glass, shadows, pills, and slogan-first headings.

## Accessibility

Keyboard operation, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, no color-only meaning, labeled mobile briefs, and reduced motion.
